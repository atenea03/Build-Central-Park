# 🏗️ Build Central Park

An optimized exterior remapping for the construction area located in front of the police station (Central Park). Ideal for roleplay servers looking to add a more realistic and dynamic atmosphere to the central zone.

![version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-v2026-orange) ![platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Windows%20%2F%20FiveM-blue) ![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-Atenea%20Store%20Tools-yellow)

---

### 📌 Main Features

* **100% Optimised** (Guaranteed performance for your server).
* **Exterior mapping** (Focused exclusively on the exterior environment).
* **Compatible with NoPixel Visual**.

---

### 🚧 Feature Details

* **It's just the outside:** No heavy interiors, strictly designed for the outer landscape.
* **Exterior decoration:** Detailed construction site and urban works props.
* **Cranes:** Inclusion of large construction cranes to add depth and realism to the skyline.
* **The entire area is fenced off:** The complete perimeter is properly enclosed with safety fencing.
* **Debris:** Debris, building materials, and decorative construction equipment placed throughout.

---

### 📥 Installation

1. Download or clone the `ate_centralpark` repository.
2. Copy the folder into your FiveM server's `resources` directory.
3. Add `ensure ate_centralpark` to your `server.cfg`.
